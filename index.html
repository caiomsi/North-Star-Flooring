What we do

Services

From a single room to a whole floor plan, for homeowners, builders and designers.

  • Hardwood installation

    Solid hardwood installed plank by plank, from subfloor to finished room.

  • Engineered wood installation

    Engineered wood planks laid with the same care as solid hardwood.

  • Luxury vinyl plank

    LVP installation for kitchens, basements and everyday living spaces.

  • Laminate installation

    Laminate flooring installed cleanly, with tight seams and tidy edges.

  • Tile installation

    Floor tile set for residential and commercial spaces.

  • Carpet installation

    Carpet installed and finished at the edges and transitions.

  • Sand & refinish

    Sanding and refinishing to bring existing hardwood floors back to life.

  • Stairs & stair casing

    Treads, risers and stair casing, including older and tricky stairs.

  • Self-leveling floor prep

    Self-leveling underlayment to give new flooring a flat, ready surface.

  • Basement remodels

    Complete basement remodels, from floor prep to finished space.

  • Repairs

    Repairs to existing floors, so you do not always have to start over.

About us

Two owners, one mission

North Star Flooring & Construction was founded by Mateus and Gilmar, with over six years of hands-on experience in flooring and construction. We are based in South Salt Lake and work across Utah.

Our name reflects our mission: to guide your floors to excellence, one project at a time. We take on new construction and renovation, and we work with homeowners, builders and designers.
